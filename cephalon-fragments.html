Cephalon Fragments

Cephalon Fragments are collectible pieces of lore scattered throughout the Origin System.

Scanning fragments unlocks Codex artwork and information about the Origin System, including details about Ordis's past.

Select a location to browse its lore entries. Each entry has its own expandable box for the corresponding lore text.
