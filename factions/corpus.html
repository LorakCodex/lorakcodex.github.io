The Corpus: Origins, Profit and the Board

The Corpus are one of the major powers in the Origin System. A merchant cult built around wealth, technology and the pursuit of profit, they combine commercial influence with a powerful military force of robotic proxies and hired personnel.

Their influence reaches across the system through industrial operations, trading networks, research facilities and the exploitation of resources. To the Corpus, almost everything has a price, including technology, territory and even life itself.

Origins of the Corpus

The Corpus trace their history to the Orokin era, when their ancestors existed within the wider social and economic structure of the Empire. Their later development transformed them into a powerful commercial organisation operating throughout the Origin System.

Their identity became closely tied to trade, technological advancement and the accumulation of wealth. Over time, the Corpus developed into a faction whose influence extends far beyond ordinary commerce.

Parvos Granum

Parvos Granum is the founder of the Corpus and one of the most important figures in their history. His rise from humble beginnings established the foundations of the Corpus philosophy and its emphasis on profit, ambition and individual achievement.

Granum's teachings became central to Corpus belief. Although the organisation grew into a vast commercial power, his original ideals and legacy continued to influence its identity.

His return and the events surrounding the Deadlock Protocol reveal that the Corpus have not always remained faithful to the principles of their founder.

The Corpus Board

The Corpus Board represents the faction's powerful commercial leadership. Its members hold considerable influence over Corpus operations, industry and military activity.

Wealth and status are central to the Board's authority. Its members pursue their own interests while competing for influence within the wider organisation.

Individual figures such as Nef Anyo and Alad V demonstrate the different ambitions and methods found within Corpus leadership.

The Cult of Profit

The Corpus are more than a conventional merchant organisation. Their beliefs are built around the worship of profit, with wealth treated as a measure of worth and success.

Their teachings influence everyday life, business practices and the treatment of those who work under Corpus authority. Debt and obligation can become instruments of control, leaving individuals trapped within systems designed to benefit the wealthy.

This ideology is particularly visible in the exploitation of workers and the harsh conditions experienced by those who cannot meet their debts.

Robotics and Technology

Corpus military strength relies heavily on advanced technology and robotic proxies. Their forces deploy automated combat units, drones, mechanical support systems and specialised weapons across the Origin System.

Robotics allow the Corpus to supplement their comparatively limited population with large numbers of machines. These units can be deployed in industrial facilities, ships and military installations.

Their research and development programmes also pursue recovered Orokin technology, often seeking to turn ancient discoveries into commercial and military advantages.

The Solaris and Fortuna

On Venus, the Corpus operate extensive industrial facilities and exploit the resources of the planet. Fortuna serves as a centre of this activity, where Solaris workers are forced to labour under Corpus control.

Many Solaris are burdened by debt and have had body parts replaced with mechanical components. Their working conditions reveal the human cost of Corpus expansion and the faction's pursuit of profit.

Solaris United emerges as a resistance movement seeking to free the workers from Corpus exploitation.

The Corpus and the Tenno

The Corpus view the Tenno and their Warframes as valuable targets. Their technology and connection to the Old War make them objects of considerable interest to Corpus researchers and commercial interests.

Their pursuit of Warframe technology has led to repeated conflicts with the Tenno. Some Corpus figures seek to capture, study or exploit the power of the Tenno, while others pursue their own ambitions through direct confrontation.

Related Quest Lore

Corpus history, leadership and exploitation are explored throughout several parts of Warframe's story.

Vox Solaris introduces the struggle of the Solaris against Corpus control on Venus, while The Deadlock Protocol explores Parvos Granum and the history of the Corpus.

The Glast Gambit also provides insight into Corpus society and the consequences of its treatment of those under its authority.
