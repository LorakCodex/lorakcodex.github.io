The Grineer: Origins, Queens and Empire

The Grineer are one of the major military powers of the Origin System in Warframe. Their empire is built around vast clone armies, military expansion and the authority of the Twin Queens.

Their history reaches back to the Orokin Empire, where Grineer ancestors served as cloned workers. Following the collapse of Orokin rule, the Grineer became a power in their own right.

Origins Under the Orokin

The Grineer originated as clones created to serve the Orokin Empire. They were used for labour and other dangerous duties, and their existence reflected the rigid hierarchy and exploitation of the Orokin era.

After the Orokin Empire fell, the Grineer were no longer simply servants of their former masters. They organised under new leadership and developed an empire of their own.

Cloning and Genetic Decay

Centuries of cloning have left the Grineer population suffering from severe genetic deterioration. Their bodies and capabilities vary, and many rely on technology, replacement parts and military equipment to continue functioning.

Their immense numbers remain a major source of power. The empire uses its clone armies to occupy territory, suppress resistance and expand its influence throughout the Origin System.

The Twin Queens

The Twin Queens stand at the centre of Grineer authority. Their rule shapes the empire's military direction and its pursuit of further territory and power.

Their connection to the Grineer's past, the Kuva and the events of The War Within reveals more about the structure of their rule and the traditions that sustain their authority.

Military Culture and Technology

Grineer society is heavily militarised. Its forces use industrial weapons, armoured units, heavy machinery and a wide range of specialised troops. Their technology often reflects a practical emphasis on durability, force and mass deployment.

Grineer units operate across the Origin System, from heavily fortified installations to planetary settlements and open battlefields. Their expansion frequently brings them into conflict with the Tenno and other inhabitants of the system.

The Grineer and the Tenno

The Grineer regard the Tenno as a threat to their expansion and authority. Their forces repeatedly clash with the Tenno across the Star Chart, while the wider conflict reveals divisions and exceptions within Grineer society.

Not every Grineer follows the same path. Individuals and groups can reject the Queens' rule, demonstrating that the empire is not made up of a single unchanging viewpoint.

Related Quest Lore

Grineer history and leadership are explored through several parts of Warframe's story, including Vor's Prize and The War Within. These quests provide additional context for the Grineer, their rulers and their place in the Origin System.
