To help establish a path to Tau, the Lotus enlisted the help of Praghasa. In order for this to work, Praghasa needed to feed, but to do so without risking the destruction of the balance, the flesh she consumed had to be regenerative. The Tenno were then tasked with collecting Moonflesh from Eris. Loid aided this effort by sending in a Vessel for the Tenno to face the Infested on the surface of Eris, providing Praghasa with her first bit of Moonflesh and allowing the Operation to begin. After the Operation concluded, Praghasa fed on Eris, leaving only the surrounding ships remaining.
With Tau in sight, Operation Taubound tasks you with acquiring regenerative Moonflesh from Eris units in regular Eris missions or by completing the Alerts that appear. The Moonflesh is then fed to Praghasa so that Praghasa can help make the path to Tau clearer.
