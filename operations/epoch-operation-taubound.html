Epoch Operation: Taubound

The Fate Of All Flesh
Tenno. We must work to keep Eris clear of Infested so that Praghasa may feed. If you harvest any viable Moonflesh from Eris, take it to Mother. She will need them. With what I am asking of her, we must be certain that this succeeds.
To help establish a path to Tau, the Lotus enlists the help of Praghasa. In order for this to work, Praghasa needs to feed, but to do so without risking the destruction of the balance, the flesh she consumes must be regenerative. The Tenno were then tasked with collecting Moonflesh from Eris. Loid aided this effort by sending in a Vessel for the Tenno to face the Infested on the surface of Eris, providing Praghasa with her first bit of Moonflesh and allowing the Operation to begin.
With Tau in sight, Operation Taubound tasks you with acquiring regenerative Moonflesh from Eris units in regular Eris missions or by completing the Alerts that appear. The Moonflesh is then fed to Praghasa so that Praghasa can help make the path to Tau clearer.
