Operation Synopsis
Operation: Eight Claw was a limited-time Operation in which Major Neci Rusalka, possessed by the Man in the Wall, took over Duviri following the events of the Hex Quest in Höllvania. The player was tasked with completing the Isleweaver game mode to earn Dominus Aureus.

Introduction
Operation: Eight Claw tasked players with completing a run of a new Duviri mission type called Isleweaver. The mission featured dialogue from Dominus Thrax and Mathila, alongside taunts from the Man in the Wall.
Oraxia
The mission included a sub-boss fight against Oraxia.
Fragmented Tide
The final fight was against the Fragmented Tide. When the Fragmented Tide reached its final health bar, another spawned, forming a duet.
Mission Complete
After both were finally defeated, players were rewarded with a chest containing resources and blueprints related to Oraxia and Duviri in general.
