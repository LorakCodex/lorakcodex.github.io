Quest Archives

• The Awakening: For centuries, the Tenno have been dormant. As the Grineer Empire grows, the Lotus starts waking them up.

• Captain Vor's Plot: Captain Vor hunts down newly awakened Tenno on Earth. Instead of killing the player like ordered by the Twin Queens, he attaches an Ascaris device to control the Warframe.

• The Intervention: The Lotus remotely surges the player's power systems, letting them break free and fight back against nearby Grineer soldiers.

Conclusion: You eventually fight Vor at the end and get picked up by Ordis, your original Orbiter Cephalon.

• The United Front: Grineer soldier Kahl-175, Corpus tech Veso-R, the Chosen Operator, and Teshin launch a desperate, coordinated assault against Erra’s Sentient armada, ultimately failing as Teshin is captured and forced to fight Erra.

Eventually, the Operator shows up, but this is also a failed effort. Ballas opens a Void portal, which begins pulling Natah through. The Operator, in an effort to pull her back, exits Transference, with the Warframe still holding on. Ballas takes advantage of this and cuts Natah’s hand off. The Operator, in shock, kneels on the ground. Ballas then stabs the Operator, sending them into the Void and trapping them in their own personal history.

• The Drifter’s Rise: With the Tenno cast into the Void and the Lotus turned into an Eidolon, a mysterious, older version of the Operator—the Drifter—leads a resistance movement against Ballas’s brainwashed empire, Narmer.

This eventually leads to the Drifter having to run from an Archon named Nira. After this, the Drifter goes to the only person he could think of who would know something about Archons, Hunhow. Despite his hatred, Hunhow puts it aside because he wants his daughter free of Ballas. To help you fight them, he gives you a bow called Nataruk.

• The Choice & Convergence: After traversing the past as the Operator, the Drifter links up with the Operator mid-fight with Eidolon Lotus. This leads to you rescuing your younger self and the Drifter gaining Void powers. You must then choose whether to face the final battle as the Operator or the Drifter.

Conclusion: You defeat Ballas, break his hold over the Origin System, and the Man in the Wall shows up expanding Ballas’s Void portal speaks his language before leaving.

When back on the Orbiter, you decide the name the Lotus will use to lead the others, though this is her choice.
