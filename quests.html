Quest Order

  • The Awakening: For centuries, the Tenno have been dormant. As the Grineer Empire grows, the Lotus starts waking them up.
  • Captain Vor's Plot: Captain Vor hunts down newly awakened Tenno on Earth. Instead of killing the player like ordered by the Twin Queens, he attaches an Ascaris device to control the Warframe.
  • The Intervention: The Lotus remotely surges the player's power systems, letting them break free and fight back against nearby Grineer soldiers.
  • The Broken Orbiter: After escaping Earth, the Tenno arrives on their personal ship only to find it heavily damaged, offline, and stripped of vital components by the Grineer.
  • The Cephalon's Return: Ordis, the ship's erratic Cephalon companion, boots back up to help the player locate and reinstall missing segments, bringing the Foundry and Arsenal back to fighting shape.
  • Darvo's Rescue: To find a cure for the burrowing Ascaris device, the Tenno intercepts Grineer comms and rescues Darvo, an imprisoned Corpus merchant who provides a blueprint for an Ascaris Negator.
  • The Anti-Tamper Trigger: You forge the Negator in your ship's Foundry, but injecting it activates a hidden Grineer failsafe, transforming the Ascaris into a ticking bomb that will detonate your Warframe.
  • The Confrontation: The Lotus tracks the detonation override signal straight to Mercury, forcing the Tenno to infiltrate a mining outpost and face Vor head-on as he fights using Void energy from his Janus Key.
  • Conclusion: You defeat Vor in combat, which permanently disables the detonator signal, purges the Ascaris from your systems, and allows you to safely take to the stars.
