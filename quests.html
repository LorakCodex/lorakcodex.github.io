Quest Archives

• The Awakening: For centuries, the Tenno have been dormant. As the Grineer Empire grows, the Lotus starts waking them up.

• Captain Vor's Plot: Captain Vor hunts down newly awakened Tenno on Earth. Instead of killing the player like ordered by the Twin Queens, he attaches an Ascaris device to control the Warframe.

• The Intervention: The Lotus remotely surges the player's power systems, letting them break free and fight back against nearby Grineer soldiers.

• Conclusion: You eventually fight Vor at the end and get picked up by Ordis, your original Orbiter Cephalon.

• The Distress Signal: The Tenno receives an ancient, encrypted Orokin transmission broadcasting an urgent distress signal from Cambion Drift on Deimos.

• The Entrati Family: Upon arrival, the player discovers The Entrati, a family of Orokin aristocrats who have been mutated and merged with the Infestation, though they retain some level of their human minds.

• The Heart’s Crisis: The family explains that the Heart of Deimos—a mechanical construct that pumps Void energy across the entire Origin System to power all Warframes—is failing due to an attack by a Necramech called Snake.

• The Reclamation: Stripped of their Warframe's power due to the failing Heart, the player must pilot Snake, the same Necramech Grandmother used to damage the Heart, then fight through the Infestation, defeat the threat, and restart the Heart to restore power to all Warframes and Void tech across the System.

• The Vision: The quest begins when a mysterious glowing orb of light appears in the player's Orbiter personal quarters, drawing the Operator into a memory or vision.

• The Lua Reservoir: The light leads the Operator to the Lua Reservoir—the chamber where the Tenno slept during the Old War. There, the player finds the Lotus standing at her Lotus pod.

• Ballas Return: The Orokin Executor, Ballas, who was long thought dead, steps out from the light. He reveals himself as one of the surviving Orokin after the Naga Drums and refers to the Lotus by the name of the woman she was reprogrammed to function as, Margulis.

• The Betrayal: Ballas severs the Lotus's mechanical connection to her Lotus pod, claiming she has been imprisoned by the Tenno. The Lotus takes Ballas's hand and willingly departs with him, abandoning the Tenno and leaving behind only her helmet.

Conclusion: You pick up Lotus’s helmet and place it in your Orbiter.

• The United Front: Grineer soldier Kahl-175, Corpus tech Veso-R, the Chosen Operator, and Teshin launch a desperate, coordinated assault against Erra’s Sentient armada, ultimately failing as Teshin is captured and forced to fight Erra.

Eventually, the Operator shows up, but this is also a failed effort. Ballas opens a Void portal, which begins pulling Natah through. The Operator, in an effort to pull her back, exits Transference, with the Warframe still holding on. Ballas takes advantage of this and cuts Natah’s hand off. The Operator, in shock, kneels on the ground. Ballas then stabs the Operator, sending them into the Void and trapping them in their own personal history.

• The Drifter’s Rise: With the Tenno cast into the Void and the Lotus turned into an Eidolon, a mysterious, older version of the Operator—the Drifter—leads a resistance movement against Ballas’s brainwashed empire, Narmer.

This eventually leads to the Drifter having to run from an Archon named Nira. After this, the Drifter goes to the only person he could think of who would know something about Archons, Hunhow. Despite his hatred, Hunhow puts it aside because he wants his daughter free of Ballas. To help you fight them, he gives you a bow called Nataruk.

• The Choice & Convergence: After traversing the past as the Operator, the Drifter links up with the Operator mid-fight with Eidolon Lotus. This leads to you rescuing your younger self and the Drifter gaining Void powers. You must then choose whether to face the final battle as the Operator or the Drifter.

Conclusion: You defeat Ballas, break his hold over the Origin System, and the Man in the Wall shows up expanding Ballas’s Void portal speaks his language before leaving.

When back on the Orbiter, you decide the name the Lotus will use to lead the others, though this is her choice.

• The Return: The Orokin colony ship, the Zariman Ten Zero, re-emerges from the Void and begins singing across the Origin System, attracting the Tenno, Grineer, and Corpus.

• The Holdfasts: Players meet The Holdfasts, the ethereal, ghost-like adult crew, the last adults of the original disaster not to go insane when the Man in the Wall interrupted the trip who exist as Conceptual Embodiments—manifestations of memories shaped by the Void.

• The Threat: Led by Quinn, the Holdfasts explain that a member named Kira has succumbed to the Void's song, mutating into a monstrous Void Angel and threatening a total breakdown of reality via a Void Cascade.

• The Conclusion: The Chosen Operator works to purge contamination, defend the ship, and defeat Kira in the Void realm to reclaim their lost home from the Void Angel.
