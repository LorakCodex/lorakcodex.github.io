A Man of Few Words

The Distress Call: The Tenno receives a message from Darvo, who asks for help rescuing his father, Frohd Bek, from a dangerous situation.

The Rescue: The Tenno follows Darvo's instructions and fights through Grineer forces to reach the location where Frohd Bek is being held.

The Reunion: The Tenno helps reunite Darvo with his father, although their strained relationship and disagreements remain clear.

The Conclusion: The rescue succeeds, and Darvo is grateful for the Tenno's assistance, adding another chapter to the complicated relationship between the Corpus merchant and his family.
