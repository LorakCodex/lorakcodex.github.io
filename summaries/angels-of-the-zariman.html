Angels of the Zariman

• The Return: The Orokin colony ship, the Zariman Ten Zero, re-emerges from the Void and begins singing across the Origin System, attracting the Tenno, Grineer, and Corpus.

• The Holdfasts: Players meet The Holdfasts, the ethereal, ghost-like adult crew, the last adults of the original disaster not to go insane when the Man in the Wall interrupted the trip who exist as Conceptual Embodiments—manifestations of memories shaped by the Void.

• The Threat: Led by Quinn, the Holdfasts explain that a member named Kira has succumbed to the Void's song, mutating into a monstrous Void Angel and threatening a total breakdown of reality via a Void Cascade.

• The Conclusion: The Chosen Operator works to purge contamination, defend the ship, and defeat Kira in the Void realm to reclaim their lost home from the Void Angel.
