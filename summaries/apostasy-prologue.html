Apostasy Prologue

• The Vision: The quest begins when a mysterious glowing orb of light appears in the player's Orbiter personal quarters, drawing the Operator into a memory or vision.

• The Lua Reservoir: The light leads the Operator to the Lua Reservoir—the chamber where the Tenno slept during the Old War. There, the player finds the Lotus standing at her Lotus pod.

• Ballas Return: The Orokin Executor, Ballas, who was long thought dead, steps out from the light. He reveals himself as one of the surviving Orokin after the Naga Drums and refers to the Lotus by the name of the woman she was reprogrammed to function as, Margulis.

• The Betrayal: Ballas severs the Lotus's mechanical connection to her Lotus pod, claiming she has been imprisoned by the Tenno. The Lotus takes Ballas's hand and willingly departs with him, abandoning the Tenno and leaving behind only her helmet.

Conclusion: You pick up Lotus’s helmet and place it in your Orbiter.
