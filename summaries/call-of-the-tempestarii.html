Call of the Tempestarii

• The Distress Signal: The Tenno receives a mysterious distress call connected to a lost Railjack known as the Tempestarii. Its unusual signal appears to be linked to a presence within the Void.

• The Haunted Railjack: While investigating the Tempestarii, the Tenno encounters visions and echoes of its past. These reveal the ship's connection to a tragic battle involving Corpus forces.

• Vala Glarios: The investigation brings the Tenno into conflict with Vala Glarios, a Corpus captain whose hatred and desire for revenge are tied to the Tempestarii's history.

• Sevagoth's Return: The Tenno discovers that the mysterious presence aboard the Tempestarii is connected to Sevagoth, a lost Warframe whose Shadow has continued to exist beyond death.

• Conclusion: The Tenno confronts Vala's forces and uncovers the fate of the Tempestarii. Sevagoth's blueprints are recovered, allowing the Tenno to rebuild the lost Warframe.
