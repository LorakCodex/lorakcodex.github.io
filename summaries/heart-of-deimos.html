Heart of Deimos

• The Distress Signal: The Tenno receives an ancient, encrypted Orokin transmission broadcasting an urgent distress signal from Cambion Drift on Deimos.

• The Entrati Family: Upon arrival, the player discovers The Entrati, a family of Orokin aristocrats who have been mutated and merged with the Infestation, though they retain some level of their human minds.

• The Heart’s Crisis: The family explains that the Heart of Deimos—a mechanical construct that pumps Void energy across the entire Origin System to power all Warframes—is failing due to an attack by a Necramech called Snake.

• The Reclamation: Stripped of their Warframe's power due to the failing Heart, the player must pilot Snake, the same Necramech Grandmother used to damage the Heart, then fight through the Infestation, defeat the threat, and restart the Heart to restore power to all Warframes and Void tech across the System.
