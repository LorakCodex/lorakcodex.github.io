Jade Shadows: Constellations

  • 1. The Paradox of Two Sons Following the events of Jade Shadows, Stalker is sent to face his sons from other timelines. Each son is fighting for who gets Stalker for themselves, with the Man in the Wall making them battle eternally.
  • 2. The Battle for the Future In their battle, they aren't alone; they are joined by Mentors. Sirius has Ryoku, a Protoframe of Ash. Orion has Vena, a Protoframe of Garuda. Each Protoframe commands a different Corpus ship. Stalker isn't alone with his dueling sons; he goes to the Operator's Orbiter and finds Excalibur Umbra, who understands the situation and jumps at the opportunity to help.
  • 3. The Void's Manipulation Stalker is sent to gain data with Umbra, and with this newfound data, Stalker is sent to face the Mentors one at a time. Following this, Stalker is sent to grab a Void Spark. This leads to Stalker facing a Mockery that copied the appearance of Jade, which Stalker has to face to gain the Void Spark.
  • 4. Conclusion To save the family and create peace, Hunhow sacrifices some of his memories in exchange for the Man in the Wall to end the battle he began. This leads to Stalker waking up in Pontis Tower and cooking for the Twins with Jade's voice in his head.
