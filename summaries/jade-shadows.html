Jade Shadows

Sorren’s Past: Before the collapse of the Orokin Empire, the Stalker was a Low Guardian named Sorren who eventually fell in love with a woman named Jade. Their romantic relationship and her subsequent pregnancy strictly violated Orokin caste laws, as Sorren admits to Jade.

The Punishment: As a cruel punishment orchestrated by Executor Ballas, both Sorren and Jade were forcibly transformed into Warframes, with Jade trapped in a state of permanent pregnancy.

The Final Moments: In the present day, Jade’s health rapidly declines. The Stalker raids Corpus ships for anything that might help save her and eventually teams up with the Tenno. Despite his hatred of them, he seeks their help. The Tenno is eventually forced to physically help Jade deliver her baby. After giving birth, Jade disintegrates. The player is then asked to name the baby boy either Sirius or Orion.

The Escape: The Stalker eventually faces a Corpus onslaught led by Captain Xeto while carrying his newborn son. Hearing the baby cry, Xeto uncharacteristically orders a ceasefire, allowing the Stalker to escape safely to Lua.

Conclusion: After the quest, Hunhow sends the Tenno the blueprint to replicate Jade. The Stalker then abandons his title as the “Shadow Stalker” and becomes the Protector Stalker, dedicating his life to fatherhood.
