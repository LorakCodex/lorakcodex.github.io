Octavia's Anthem

• The Mandachord: Cephalon Suda asks the Tenno to recover the components of the Mandachord, an ancient musical instrument connected to Octavia. After assembling it, Suda begins experiencing strange memory lapses.

• The Song Fragments: The Tenno follows the music to ancient Orokin instruments on Uranus and Lua, recovering fragments of Octavia's Anthem. As the investigation continues, it becomes clear that a hostile presence is interfering with Suda's memories.

• Hunhow's Influence: The Sentient Hunhow has infiltrated Suda and is using her to pursue his own plans. The Tenno must use the Mandachord to reconstruct the anthem and help restore Suda's memories.

• The Datascape: With Ordis and Cephalon Simaris assisting, the Tenno enters Suda's corrupted digital realm and uses Octavia's Anthem to overcome Hunhow's influence.

• Conclusion: Suda is freed from Hunhow's control, though she suffers some short-term memory loss. In gratitude, she shares her archives about Octavia with the Tenno.
