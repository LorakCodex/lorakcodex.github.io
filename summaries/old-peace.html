• The Ceasefire: Through the Dark Refractory pool in Albrecht's laboratory, the Tenno explore memories of a time when the Orokin and Sentients attempted peace rather than total destruction.

• Xenoflora: The Tau System had plants called Xenoflora that granted Sentients individuality and free will from the Sentient Hivemind.

• The Betrayal: The peace was a strategic setup. Orokin Executor Nitokh funded the Anarchs, and Ballas manipulated events behind the scenes.

• The quest leads to a tragic climax where Ballas alters the treaty's kill-codes to terraform and incinerate the Xenoflora. This act turns the flowers to ash, ending the attempted peace treaty.

• Adis and the Operator: Subsequently, with the flowers destroyed, Adis begins attacking the Operator, ending with the Operator being fatally wounded and Adis presumably dying while healing the Operator's injuries.

• Conclusion: When an enraged Tenno tries to kill Ballas in retaliation for the manipulation, destroying the Academy in the process, they instead find Margulis, who dodges their questions and then puts them to sleep in preparation to take them back to the Origin System. She eventually wipes their memories of the incident to protect them from the wrath of the Seven Executors, taking the treason charge upon herself.
