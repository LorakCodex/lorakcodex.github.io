Old Peace

• The Ceasefire: Through the Dark Refractory pool in Albrecht's laboratory, the Tenno explore memories of a time when the Orokin and Sentients attempted peace rather than total destruction.

• Xenoflora: The Tau System had plants called Xenoflora that granted Sentients individuality and free will from the Sentient Hivemind.

• The Betrayal: The peace was a strategic setup. Orokin Executor Nitokh funded the Anarchs and Ballas to sabotage the peace between the Orokin and Sentients.

• Adis: Adis and the other Anarchs played a role in the events that destroyed the possibility of lasting peace.

• Margulis: Margulis's story connects to the wider history of the Orokin and the events that shaped the Old War.
