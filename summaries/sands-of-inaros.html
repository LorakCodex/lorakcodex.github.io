Sands of Inaros

The Mysterious Relic: The Tenno acquires an ancient vessel from Baro Ki'Teer, who tells them of a legend surrounding the forgotten Warframe Inaros.

The Ancient Trials: By completing challenges linked to the vessel's inscriptions, the Tenno uncovers more of the legend and gathers the remains needed to reconstruct Inaros.

The Tomb: The Tenno explores an ancient tomb on Mars and discovers the story of Inaros, a protector who defended the people of a Martian colony from a tyrannical ruler and his forces.

The Conclusion: The Tenno faces the tomb's guardian and completes the vessel's trials, recovering Inaros's components and learning of the Warframe's enduring legacy as a protector.
