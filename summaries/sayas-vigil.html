Saya's Vigil

• The Investigation: The Tenno arrives in Cetus, where Konzu asks for help investigating the disappearance of his old friend Onkko, Saya's husband.

• Saya's Memories: The Tenno works with Saya to uncover clues about Onkko's disappearance. Their investigation reveals that Saya's memories and her relationship with Onkko are tied to a mystery surrounding the Plains of Eidolon.

• The Grineer Operation: The search leads to a hidden Grineer operation involving a mysterious object connected to the Unum, the enormous Tower that protects Cetus.

• The Discovery: The Tenno uncovers the connection between the Grineer activity, the mysterious object, and the Eidolon phenomenon. The investigation also reveals more about Saya's past and Onkko's fate.

• Conclusion: The immediate investigation comes to an end, while the mysteries of the Unum and the Plains of Eidolon remain part of the wider story.
