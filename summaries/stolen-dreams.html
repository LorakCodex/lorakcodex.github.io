Stolen Dreams

The Investigation: The Tenno investigates mysterious artifacts known as the Arcane Codices, which have attracted the attention of both the Corpus and the Grineer.

The Rivalry: The Tenno follows the trail of the artifacts, competing with Corpus and Grineer forces who are searching for the same Orokin secrets.

The Discovery: The investigation reveals that the artifacts contain clues connected to hidden Orokin knowledge and the mysterious past of the Origin System.

The Conclusion: The Tenno recovers the Codices and uncovers evidence of a larger mystery, setting the stage for further investigations into Orokin secrets.
