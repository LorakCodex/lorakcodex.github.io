Mask of the Revenant

• The Mask: The Tenno obtains a mysterious mask connected to the lost history of the Revenant Warframe and the people of Cetus.

• The Unfinished Story: By investigating the mask and speaking with Nakak, the Tenno uncovers a legend involving the Eidolon and a mysterious guardian who once protected the Plains of Eidolon.

• The Revenant: The investigation leads the Tenno to discover traces of the Revenant Warframe, whose connection to the Eidolon transformed it into something strange and dangerous.

• Conclusion: The Tenno follows the clues surrounding the Revenant's past and recovers the blueprints needed to reconstruct the Warframe.
