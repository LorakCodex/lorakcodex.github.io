The Deadlock Protocol

• The Corpus Founder: The Tenno investigates reports surrounding Parvos Granum, the long-lost founder of the Corpus, who has returned after surviving within the Void.

• Nef Anyo's Ambition: Nef Anyo attempts to exploit Parvos's return and secure his position within the Corpus. The Tenno uncovers the conflict surrounding the founder's legacy.

• The Granum Void: The Tenno enters the Granum Void, a pocket of the Void created through Parvos's technology, where the Solaris people known as the Errant Specters are trapped.

• Protea's Secret: The investigation reveals Protea, a Warframe who once served Parvos as his personal protector. Her connection to the Granum Void becomes central to the unfolding conflict.

• Conclusion: The Tenno confronts the dangers surrounding Parvos's legacy and uncovers the origins of the technology behind the Granum Void, recovering the blueprints needed to construct Protea.
