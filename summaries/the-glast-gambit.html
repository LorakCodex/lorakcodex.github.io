The Glast Gambit

The Request: Ergo Glast, leader of the Perrin Sequence, asks the Tenno to help the Myconian colony on Eris, whose survival depends on a dangerous bargain with the Infestation.

The Myconians: The Tenno discovers that the colony has been forced to sacrifice one of its children, Nidus, to obtain a resource needed to survive. The situation becomes more urgent when the child Rell is threatened.

The Gambit: To save the colony, the Tenno enters a series of high-stakes Index matches against Nef Anyo, using the winnings and a carefully planned wager to challenge his control over the situation.

The Conclusion: The Tenno outmanoeuvres Nef Anyo and helps secure the Myconians' future, while uncovering the desperate measures the colony has taken to survive.
