The Jordas Precept

The Distress Signal: The Tenno receives a distress call from a Cephalon named Jordas, who claims to be stranded and in need of rescue.

The Investigation: Following Jordas's instructions, the Tenno discovers that an Infested outbreak has consumed a Corpus ship and transformed it into a dangerous hive.

The Betrayal: The Tenno learns that Jordas has been fused with the Infestation and is attempting to lure the Tenno into becoming part of the hive as well.

The Conclusion: The Tenno confronts and destroys the Infested Golem formed from Jordas and the Infestation, ending the immediate threat and unlocking the route to the Jordas Golem assassination.
