The Lotus Eaters

• Summons to Deimos: The Tenno investigates a mysterious disturbance at the edge of the Void expanse inside the Sanctum Anatomica.

• The Lotus's Struggle: The Lotus waits by the abyss holding a ringing 1999-era pager (KinePage) while singing to drown out the seductive, overwhelming pull of the Great Indifference.

• A Dual Appearance: Both the Operator and the Drifter appear simultaneously in the scene. due to the Void being able to handle both. Because Lotus knows the Operator is vulnerable to the Man in the Wall's influence, the Lotus instructs only the Drifter to answer the call.

• Warped to Höllvania: Interacting with the pager transports the Drifter's consciousness into the abandoned shopping mall in the year 1999.

• Following Kalymos: Inside the quiet mall, the Drifter encounters Albrecht Entrati’s pet Kavat, Kalymos, who guides them through the corridors to another ringing KinePage.

• A Temporal Connection: Answering this second device pulls the Drifter back to the present, establishing a temporal connection to the past.
