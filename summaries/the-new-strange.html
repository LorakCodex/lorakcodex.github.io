The New Strange

The Investigation: The Tenno assists Cephalon Simaris in investigating a mysterious transmission and tracking down unusual targets connected to his research.

The Sanctuary: Simaris reveals his work on the Sanctuary, a project dedicated to preserving knowledge through digital scans of living beings.

The Discovery: The investigation leads to the discovery of a mysterious Warframe, later identified as Chroma, whose actions are connected to a strange signal and the destruction of research sites.

The Conclusion: The Tenno tracks Chroma's movements and helps Simaris uncover more about the mysterious Warframe, unlocking access to the Sanctuary and its Synthesis activities.
