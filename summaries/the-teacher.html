The Teacher

• The Interruption: you track down an Orokin Mod Segment, only to be intercepted by Teshin, a surviving Dax warrior.

• The Lesson: Disapproving of the Lotus hiding your memories of the Old War, Teshin captures you to teach you how to use mods to amplify your gear.

• Corpus Ambush: Corpus forces interrupt the training, and Teshin surrenders himself so you can escape with the segment.

• The Rescue: You upgrade your mods and save Teshin from a Corpus ship, proving to him you are the Tenno he once knew.

• Conclusion: Teshin decides to work with the Lotus and moves into the Relay as your Teacher.
