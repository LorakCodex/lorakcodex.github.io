The Waverider

• The Comic: The Tenno discovers a comic book about Yareli, a legendary Ventkid who used a K-Drive to stand up to the forces controlling Fortuna.

• Roky's Story: Roky shares the comic's story with the Tenno. Its pages describe Yareli's courage and her efforts to protect the people of Fortuna from the Corpus.

• The Trials: The Tenno completes a series of K-Drive challenges inspired by the comic, progressing through its story and learning more about Yareli's legend.

• The Rescue: The story leads the Tenno to a hidden chamber where Yareli has been preserved in a glass case. The Tenno frees her, bringing the legendary Warframe's story into the present.

• Conclusion: The Tenno recovers Yareli's blueprints and helps restore the legend of the Waverider, giving the Ventkids a symbol of hope and resistance.
