The Waverider

• The Comic: Roky discovers Waverider #1, a rare Graphica telling the story of Yareli, a legendary Warframe who inspired the Ventkids and their love of K-Driving. After Roky is left comatose from injuring her neuro-conduit while trying to unlock the comic, Boon asks the Tenno to finish what she started.

• Roky’s Story: As the Tenno unlocks the comic’s pages, Boon narrates Yareli’s story. The Graphica depicts Yareli confronting Vulgran, a slave-driver who forces children to work assembling Grav-lifters. Yareli challenges him, giving the children hope and inspiring them to stand together.

• The Trials: The Tenno completes a series of K-Drive challenges to unlock the comic’s five pages, gradually revealing Yareli’s story. Meanwhile, Nef Anyo pressures the Ventkids to hand over the completed Graphica in exchange for resolving their debts and helping Roky.

• The Rescue: After the comic is completed, Boon discovers that the mysterious buyer on Deimos is Grandmother. She gives the Ventkids an elixir that restores Roky’s neuro-conduit, allowing her to awaken. Grandmother also purchases the ventilation shafts where the Ventkids live, freeing them from their debts to Nef Anyo.

Conclusion: Grandmother sends the Tenno a message explaining what she has done and sends them Yareli’s blueprint. She asks the Tenno to build Yareli and don the Yareli Warframe when visiting the Ventkids, in the hope that the children will enjoy meeting the legendary Waverider herself.
