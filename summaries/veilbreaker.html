Veilbreaker

• Kahl's Freedom: Following the New War, Kahl-175 breaks free from Narmer's control and returns to the fight against the remnants of the Sentient-led regime.

• The Rescue Missions: Working with the Lotus, Kahl undertakes dangerous missions to rescue captured Grineer brothers and sabotage Narmer facilities. His determination to protect his fellow soldiers sets him apart from the forces that once controlled him.

• The Resistance: Kahl establishes a base of operations and continues disrupting Narmer's activities. Along the way, he gains allies and uncovers the continuing threat posed by the regime.

• Pazuul's Rise: The aftermath of the New War reveals that Erra's identity and influence have become entangled with Pazuul, a figure seeking to maintain control over Narmer's remaining forces.

• Conclusion: Kahl continues his campaign against Narmer, creating a new path for himself and his brothers while the wider conflict surrounding Pazuul remains unresolved.
