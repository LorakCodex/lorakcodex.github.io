Historical Timeline

A chronological record of the major events that shaped the Origin System, from the earliest known history to the events surrounding 1999.

Pre-Orokin Era
  • XX99 is a largely unknown period of human history.
  • Humanity has not yet entered the Orokin Era.
  • Much of this period remains undocumented, although some details can be learned through the KIM conversations.
  • 1999 is a significant period of pre-Orokin human history.
Orokin Era
  • The Orokin rise to become the upper-class royalty of the Empire.
  • They create Cephalons, the Grineer, and train selected soldiers to become Dax.
  • The Orokin discover the Void and begin studying it, eventually leading to the invention of Void technology.
  • They construct the Solar Rail network, allowing travel throughout the Origin System.
  • Parvos Granum establishes the Corpus, creating the first true rival to the Orokin.
  • The Orokin create the Sentients, sending them to Tau to terraform its worlds and construct an Extrasolar Rail.
  • The Sentients successfully complete their mission, but the journey to Tau changes them.
  • After reaching Tau, the Sentients realise that the Orokin would eventually arrive and ruin what they had created.
  • The Sentients rebel against the Orokin, triggering the start of the Great Sentient War, which lasts for an unspecified amount of time.
  • The Orokin construct the Zariman 10-0 as part of an attempt to reach Tau and beat the Sentients there.
  • The Zariman 10-0 disappears into the Void in an event later known as the Zariman Incident.
The Old War and Old Peace
  • The Sentients' ability to adapt and interfere with Orokin technology means that the more advanced the Orokin become, the greater their losses. In response, the Orokin do the opposite of advancing their technology and return to the old ways of gun and blade.
  • The Orokin deploy Necramechs, Infested creatures, Grineer clones, and Warframe bio-drones in response, but all are eventually deemed unsuccessful.
  • The Zariman 10-0 eventually returns from the Void, but no adults survive the attempted jump to the Tau System.
  • The surviving children are permanently altered by their exposure to the Void.
  • The Orokin discover that the children can control the Warframes through Transference, leading Ballas to take control of the project and weaponize it.
  • The Zariman children become known as the Tenno and fight alongside their Warframes against the Sentients.
  • A brief period of peace follows as the conflict temporarily subsides.
  • The Orokin Empire, including the Tenno, travels to Tau through the Extrasolar Rail.
  • The Tenno are forced to fight a rebellion group known as the Anarchs, who are secretly funded by Executor Nitokh in an attempt to keep the war going.
  • Executor Ballas manipulates the Tenno into destroying the Xenoflora on Tau's moon, Perita, contributing to the Old War resuming.
  • The Operator is forced to fight Adis.
  • Adis fatally wounds the Operator.
  • Adis uses his ability to heal the Operator, saving their life.
  • The effort of healing the Operator ultimately causes Adis to die.
  • The Operator enters a rage and takes control of Uriel, flying to the Tauron Academy and destroying anything in sight.
  • The Operator attempts to hunt down Ballas, but Ballas is already long gone.
  • The Operator eventually encounters Margulis.
  • Margulis refuses to answer the Operator's questions.
  • Margulis puts the Operator to sleep, preparing to return them to the Origin System.
  • The Operator is placed within the Second Dream.
  • Margulis is charged with treason, taking the Operator's charge upon herself.
  • Margulis is executed by Jade Light.
  • Following Margulis' death, Ballas betrays the Orokin and begins working with Hunhow.
  • Ballas gives Hunhow the location of Lua.
  • Ballas reprograms Hunhow's daughter, Natah, beginning the chain of events that leads to her becoming the Lotus.
  • After prolonged fighting, the Tenno eventually beat the Sentients back, forcing them to retreat into hiding within the Veil.
  • As the Orokin gather to celebrate their victory, the Tenno turn against them and slaughter them under the instructions of the reprogrammed Natah.
The Collapse
  • The remaining Orokin are hunted down by the Tenno, leaving only a small number of survivors.
  • Much of the Orokin's technology becomes unusable due to its gene-locks.
  • Lua is phased into the Void in an attempt to protect it.
  • The process exposes the Lotus to the Void.
  • To survive the exposure, Lotus makes a deal that allows her to survive the effects of the Void.
  • The Infestation spreads throughout the System.
  • The Grineer, led by an unusually intelligent mutant, begin to emerge as a major power.
  • The Twin Sisters eventually become the Grineer Queens.
  • A rogue Sentient arrives on Earth and destroys almost all of the remaining Orokin Towers.
  • Only the Tower of the Unum survives.
  • The Sentient is defeated by Gara.
  • The Tenno enter cryosleep, disappearing from the System.
Post-Collapse
  • Humanity enters a prolonged dark age lasting at least a millennium.
  • Surviving factions fight over remnants of lost Orokin technology.
  • Isolated colonies struggle to survive due to the Tenno no longer being around to help.
  • The space-faring Ostrons establish a settlement near the Tower of the Unum, which becomes Cetus.
Modern Era
  • The Twin Queens order the recently demoted Captain Vor to locate and destroy the dormant Tenno cryopods. He disobeys this command and instead attempts to kidnap the Warframe using an Ascaris.
  • Vor's mission prompts the Lotus to begin awakening the Tenno.
  • The Infestation is released by Grineer scientist Dr. Tengus, causing the previously contained Infested to begin spreading across the System.
  • Deimos, concealed within the Void, returns to real-space.
  • The Entrati family and their forgotten Orokin secrets are revealed.
  • The Grineer-Corpus War erupts and continues throughout much of the modern era.
  • Alad V attempts to use the Infested to seize control of the System.
  • His plan fails, and he eventually turns to the Tenno for help with his Infestation.
  • Tyl Regor discovers the tomb of Hunhow.
  • Hunhow awakens and attempts to exterminate the Tenno.
  • Hunhow's actions result in Lua returning to real-space, and the Tenno remembering that they weren't the Warframe, but a child piloting the Warframe from a distance through Transference.
  • The Elder Queen attempts to use Continuity on the Chosen Operator, using them as a Yuvan, but the attempt fails and the Elder Queen is killed.
The New War
  • Ballas emerges from hiding and abducts the Lotus, triggering the return of her Sentient programming.
  • Ballas takes control of the remaining Sentient forces.
  • He launches a full-scale invasion of the Origin System, but fails to take Deimos after Vome and Fass blast the Murex out of the sky.
  • The Sentients defeat the combined Grineer and Corpus forces in the Veil Proxima.
  • Ballas imprisons the Chosen Operator within their own past.
  • With the Tenno neutralised, Ballas conquers the Origin System.
  • He establishes Narmer, creating an empire centred around himself.
  • Ballas rules Narmer for an undefined period of months or years.
  • The Drifter, an alternate version of the Chosen Operator, arrives in the Origin System.
  • The Drifter helps the Operator escape.
  • The Operator and Drifter ultimately become united as a single individual.
  • Together, they confront Ballas.
  • Ballas opens a portal toward Tau, tearing a hole through reality.
  • The Chosen Operator defeats Ballas.
  • The Lotus kills Ballas and closes the portal.
Post-New War / 1999 / The Hex
  • Narmer remnants remain active under Pazuul.
  • The Tenno and Kahl-175 continue fighting Narmer forces.
  • The Zariman 10-0 suddenly appears in the Origin System once again.
  • The Tenno encounter the Holdfasts and fight to protect the returning ship.
  • The enormous portal created by Ballas greatly empowers the Indifference.
  • The Murmur attacks Albrecht Entrati's secret laboratories beneath Deimos.
  • The Kalymos Sequence is activated, drawing the Tenno into the laboratories.
  • The Tenno fight the Murmur and awaken Loid, Albrecht's assistant.
  • The Drifter and Operator travel to the Sanctum, where they find Lotus at the edge of the Void.
  • Lotus sends the Drifter on a mission to retrieve Arthur's book page.
  • Lotus explains that the Drifter must travel to 1999 instead of the Operator, because the Man in the Wall considers the Operator refusing to give it the page during the New War to be a breach of their deal, making the Operator's involvement a greater risk.
  • The Hex begins with Arthur searching for his motorbike keys, as he intends to find Albrecht.
  • Arthur eventually gets a lead after fighting the tank. Following the lead eventually takes him to Albrecht, but Scaldra forces arrive and take Albrecht captive.
  • Arthur travels through the Techrot, eventually reaching Helminth, which spits the Drifter out into 1999 while they are controlling an Excalibur.
  • Arthur and the Drifter engage in a duel. The Drifter exits Transference and attempts to enter Arthur's mind, but Arthur is able to reject the attempt.
  • Arthur ultimately wins the duel by stabbing the Drifter in the hand.
  • Following the duel, the Drifter is questioned by the Hex.
  • The Drifter begins carrying out tasks for the Hex across various nodes throughout 1999.
  • One of these tasks involves finding Albrecht Entrati, but when the Drifter reaches the location, Entrati is no longer there and has instead been replaced by the Man in the Wall.
  • Eventually, the Drifter discovers the Backroom, an Albrecht Entrati safehouse hidden within the Höllvania Central Mall.
  • Inside, the Drifter finds a recording from Albrecht explaining what has happened and telling the Drifter what they must do next.
  • Eventually, the Drifter and the Hex confront Major Neci Rusalka, who has become increasingly influenced by the Man in the Wall.
  • Following the confrontation, Albrecht tells the Drifter that Rusalka has become a breach through which the Man in the Wall is entering the time loop.
  • Albrecht orders the Drifter to kill Rusalka, but the Drifter refuses.
  • The Man in the Wall kidnaps the Drifter, taking them to the Zariman 10-0.
  • The events surrounding the Zariman 10-0 lead into another cycle of 1999.
  • The Drifter eventually returns to 1999, continuing the events surrounding the Hex.
