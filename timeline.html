Timeline of the Origin System

1. Pre-Orokin Era
  • Early civilizations develop throughout the Origin System.
  • The Orokin eventually rise to power.
2. Orokin Era
  • The Orokin rise to become the ruling class of the Origin System.
  • The Grineer, Cephalons and Dax already exist as parts of Orokin society.
  • The Orokin discover how to harness and control the Void.
  • Their mastery of the Void allows the Orokin Empire to expand.
  • The Sentients are created to terraform the Tau System.
  • The Zariman 10-0 is created and eventually disappears into the Void.
  • The Zariman eventually returns, but no adults survive.
  • The surviving children develop extraordinary Void abilities.
3. The Old War
  • The Sentients return from Tau and rebel against the Orokin, beginning the Old War.
  • The Orokin develop the Warframes and Tenno to fight the Sentients.
  • Old Peace occurs as a temporary ceasefire during the conflict, similar to the Christmas Truce of World War I.
  • The war resumes after the ceasefire.
  • Margulis is murdered by the Orokin.
  • The Tenno eventually turn the tide of the war.
  • The surviving Sentients flee into the Veil, and the Orokin celebrate what they believe is victory.
4. The Collapse
  • Ballas betrays the Orokin.
  • The Tenno turn against the Orokin.
  • The Orokin Empire collapses.
  • Lotus hides the Tenno and places them into cryosleep.
  • Lua is phased into the Void, and Lotus makes a deal to survive the Void exposure.
5. Post-Collapse
  • The Origin System falls into chaos.
  • The Grineer and Corpus become major powers.
  • The Tenno gradually awaken.
  • Hunhow's actions return Lua to real space.
  • The Tenno discover that they are actually children controlling the Warframes remotely.
6. Modern Era
  • The Tenno continue fighting across the Origin System.
  • The Infestation is released by Grineer scientist Dr. Tengus, and the previously contained Infested begin spreading across the System.
  • The Tenno uncover more of the Orokin's secrets.
  • Major conflicts eventually lead toward the New War.
7. The New War
  • The Sentients invade the Origin System.
  • Ballas takes control.
  • The Tenno fight to stop the invasion.
  • Ballas is defeated and the New War ends.
  • The Drifter becomes increasingly important to the events that follow.
8. Post-New War / 1999 / The Hex
  • The Drifter becomes involved in the events surrounding 1999.
  • The Drifter encounters The Hex.
  • The Drifter is questioned by the Hex and begins working with them.
  • One of their tasks involves finding Albrecht Entrati.
  • When the Drifter arrives, they instead encounter the Man in the Wall.
  • Events involving Rusalka, Entrati and the Man in the Wall escalate.
  • The Drifter eventually refuses to kill Rusalka when ordered to do so.
  • The Drifter is kidnapped by the Man in the Wall, leading into the next chapter of the story.
