Vor's Prize

• The Broken Orbiter: After escaping Earth, the Tenno arrives on their personal ship only to find it heavily damaged, offline, and stripped of vital components by the Grineer.

• The Cephalon's Return: Ordis, the ship's erratic companion, boots back up to help the player locate and reinstall missing segments, bringing the Foundry and Arsenal back to fighting shape.

• Darvo's Rescue: To find a cure for the burrowing Ascaris device, the Tenno intercepts Grineer comms and rescues Darvo, an imprisoned Corpus merchant who provides a blueprint for an Ascaris Negator.

• The Anti-Tamper Trigger: You forge the Negator in your ship's Foundry, but injecting it activates a hidden Grineer failsafe, transforming the Ascaris into a ticking bomb that will detonate your Warframe.

• The Confrontation: The Lotus tracks the detonation override signal straight to Mercury, forcing the Tenno to infiltrate a mining outpost and face Vor head-on as he fights using Orokin Void energy from his Janus Key.

• Conclusion: You defeat Vor in combat, which permanently disables the detonator signal, purges the Ascaris from your systems, and allows you to safely act as a Tenno without the risk of Vor’s Ascaris taking over your systems.
